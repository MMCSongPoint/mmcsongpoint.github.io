Chords on slides

The app reads the words on each slide and puts the chord sheet's chords above the music. Musicians see them when they follow along with View Slides, in the key they pick. Songs marked Needs checking are hidden from musicians until you open them, fix anything that's off and tap ✓ Looks right. Keep this screen open while it places them (about 20 seconds a song).

Put each chord in square brackets just before the word or syllable it's played on: [G]Amazing [G7]grace. Put a part's name on its own line (Verse 1, Chorus). The key goes in {key: G}. Saving changes it for everyone; the old version is kept in Drive (Chord Sheet Backups).
